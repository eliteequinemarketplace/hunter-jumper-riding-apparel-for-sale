Elite Equine Marketplace lists hunter jumper riding apparel from verified sellers — show coats, tall boots, helmets, breeches, and show clothes at every price point from everyday schooling gear through top-of-the-line competition apparel.

Used Show Coats — Significant Savings Over Retail

Quality hunter jumper show coats cost $400–$1,500 or more new. The used show coat market offers substantial savings — often 40–60% below retail — for buyers willing to purchase a gently used coat in excellent condition. Filter by size, color, condition, and price to find your perfect coat.

Used Tall Boots for Sale

Custom and off-the-shelf tall boots from Parlanti, Konigs, Ariat Volant, and Effingham can cost $500–$3,000 or more new. Quality used tall boots offer exceptional value — always check calf and height measurements carefully when purchasing used boots online.

Important: Helmets Should Always Be Purchased New

Elite Equine Marketplace only lists new riding helmets. Used helmets that may have experienced a fall should never be resold — always purchase a new helmet and replace after any impact.
